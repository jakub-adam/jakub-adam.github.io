PUT YOUR PICTURES HERE

profile.jpg            your photo for the home page (square or portrait, at least 800 px, face in the upper half)
anything-else.jpg      project screenshots or news pictures

To use a picture for a project or news post, open src/data/site.js and add a line like
    img: 'images/my-screenshot.jpg'
inside that project or post.

Keep file names simple: lowercase, no spaces.
